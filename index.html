      Client Care

      How do I order using my game points?

      Add a product to your bag, then switch on Use points in the bag or at checkout. Drag the slider or tap Max to choose how many points to spend — 20 points removes RM1. The discount and new total appear immediately and are written into the WhatsApp order message. Points are deducted only when you actually send the order.

      How much is one point worth?

      One point is RM0.05. Twenty points equal RM1. You can spend points on the product subtotal, up to the full subtotal — delivery is charged separately unless your subtotal reaches RM100.

      How do I earn points?

      Clear a colour lot in the Baéq Arcade and press Claim points. Faster finishes, longer combos and higher difficulty pay more — up to 120 points per round. Confirmed orders may also receive a manual bonus after payment.

      Do my points expire or disappear?

      Points stay in your wallet on this device until you spend them. If you close checkout without sending the order, nothing is deducted.

      How do I spin the figurine?

      Drag the figurine left or right to rotate it, scroll to zoom, and use the Look Studio rail underneath to change spin speed, lighting, glow, skin tone and more.

      How long does delivery take?

      Usually 2 to 4 working days for Peninsular Malaysia, and 4 to 7 days for Sabah and Sarawak.

      Can I exchange or return an item?

      Yes, if the item is damaged or the wrong one was sent. Contact us within 3 days of receiving it.

      Which payment methods do you accept?

      Bank transfer, DuitNow and e-wallets. We’ll send the details through WhatsApp.
