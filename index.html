    Client Care

    How do I order?

    Tap the Order button on a product. WhatsApp opens with a message already written. Send it to us and we’ll confirm stock and payment.

    How do I choose a size?

    Most of our inner caps are free size and stretch. If you’re unsure, ask us on WhatsApp.

    How long does delivery take?

    Usually 2 to 4 working days for Peninsular Malaysia, and 4 to 7 days for Sabah and Sarawak.

    Can I exchange or return an item?

    Yes, if the item is damaged or the wrong one was sent. Contact us within 3 days of receiving it.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Bank transfer, DuitNow and e-wallets. We’ll send the details through WhatsApp.
