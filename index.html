      Client Care

      How do I order?

      Tap Add to bag on a product, then Continue to checkout. WhatsApp opens with the order already written. Send it to us and we’ll confirm stock and payment.

      How do arcade points work?

      Finish a colour lot in the Baéq Arcade and press Tuntut mata. The points are added to your Baéq Circle balance on this device immediately, and can be redeemed for checkout rewards.

      How do I choose a size?

      Most of our inner caps are free size and stretch. If you’re unsure, ask us on WhatsApp.

      How long does delivery take?

      Usually 2 to 4 working days for Peninsular Malaysia, and 4 to 7 days for Sabah and Sarawak.

      Can I exchange or return an item?

      Yes, if the item is damaged or the wrong one was sent. Contact us within 3 days of receiving it.

      Which payment methods do you accept?

      Bank transfer, DuitNow and e-wallets. We’ll send the details through WhatsApp.

          Setup gambar produk

          Gambar kena berada dalam repo GitHub awak. Senarai di bawah tunjuk nama fail tepat yang website cari, dan status live sama ada fail itu sudah ada.

          1. Dalam repo GitHub: Add file → Create new file, taip images/.gitkeep, kemudian Commit.
          2. Masuk folder images → Add file → Upload files → drag gambar ikut nama di bawah → Commit.
          3. Tunggu deploy (~1 minit). Refresh dengan Ctrl + Shift + R. Nama fail mesti sebiji sama — huruf besar/kecil penting.
